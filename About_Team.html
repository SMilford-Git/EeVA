The Team

Meet the Team

EeVA has been developed by an interdisciplinary team working across bioethics, genome informatics, automation, and responsible AI. The project brings together ethical reflection and technical expertise to support clearer, more careful evaluation of emerging technologies.

Dr Stephen R. Milford

Bioethicist · AI in health

Stephen is a bioethicist at the Institute for Biomedical Ethics, University of Basel. His work focuses on AI in healthcare, biomedical ethics, human identity, and responsible technology development.
